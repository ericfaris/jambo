Board — turn plaque, seat block, tableau (2026-10-12)

The brass turn plaque sits between deck and discard (CenterRow); -mine on your turn, -compact on phones. Coins in carved sockets count actions; a spent one is dark. The seat block (SeatInfo) is the same for you, a trader and a friend: portrait (trader art, your avatar, or the silhouette), name, minted coin + gold, card count.

Utility states

Ready (a slow ember), used (turned sideways like a tapped card, art still visible), and unusable right now (faded, with the engine's reason as the tooltip — hover it). When a utility is used it turns sideways in the spotlight in place; it doesn't fly anywhere.

Opponent-play spotlight

A trader's (or friend's) play is held center stage for a moment: portrait, headline, detail and — for traders only — a quote. Friends get their name and avatar, never a quote or voice.

Flights

A flight starts and lands on a real place — a pile, a hand, a utility row, a market slot, or the card that owns the effect. Flying cards are real card faces (.flight-card .card-face), never bare art. Click to fly.

Resolve panels — buttons, cancel, auction

The linen resolve body (.resolve-body) with ware picks, the round Cancel ✕ (.panel-cancel-x) — you can back out from any of the card's screens until the friend or trader is involved (a Guard decision, a bid, a draft pick, an opponent choice) — and the auction board, whose leader line says who leads by name.

Landing — CTA and modes

One gold button; modes named by task, hung as wooden market signs (sway on their ropes, swing on hover, drop in on load). Play a friend never asks about a saved game (it doesn't touch it). How to play / Settings / Sound are brass icon buttons with a tag on hover. The game app draws real SVG icons; this page uses glyphs.

Play a friend — lobby, presence, leaving

The lobby (FriendLobby, all .friend-lobby-*) is the landing plate: code in gold digits, the invite link with Copy (Share where the browser has it), a waiting line, a guest note, Cancel. On the board the friend sits in the trader's seat with a presence dot; a fixed banner (never in the layout) says when they drop, counts down their held seat, and says when they're back.
